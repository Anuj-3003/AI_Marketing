● Yes — unbounded scraping is a real risk here, especially for large advertisers
  running hundreds of ads. I'd cap it at the scraper level (e.g., most recent
  20-30 ads per source per competitor, sorted by first_seen/recency) rather than
  scraping everything and truncating later, since that also bounds Playwright
  runtime and LLM context size when you later feed these into ad generation. The
  main tradeoff is recency vs. completeness — you might miss an older evergreen
  ad that's still performing well, but for a demo app recency is the better
  proxy for "what they're actively running now."
